Quick-Pad

Created by HattyHats

QuickPad is a hyper-fast, serverless scratchpad that syncs instantly across your devices. It was built to eliminate the friction of modern note-taking: no logins, no paywalls, and no data harvesting. You just open the link and type.

Core Features:

  • 🥷 Zero-Knowledge URLs: Invisible AES-256 encryption keys are built right into the URL hash. The server only ever sees scrambled ciphertext.
  • 🔗 Read-Only Links: Share a special `?view=` link that allows friends to watch you type live but disables their ability to edit.
  • 🧘 Zen Mode: Hide all distractions and menus for a 100% full-screen writing environment.
  • ⚡ Instant Sync: Share your URL to collaborate in real-time.
  • 💬 Secure DMs: True RSA-encrypted direct messaging. Click a name to chat!
  • 🌧️ Matrix Screensaver: Customizable idle animation engine.
  • 🔥 Burn Notes: Create self-destructing links that erase forever after reading.
  • 📴 True Offline Mode: Keep typing without Wi-Fi. Syncs intelligently when back online.

Privacy & Security Architecture:

QuickPad is engineered for total anonymity. It uses a serverless architecture acting purely as a relay. Your IP address is never shared with other users, meaning you can safely share links with strangers on the internet. Direct messages are encrypted using Military-Grade RSA-OAEP 2048-bit cryptography before leaving your device, making it mathematically impossible for anyone (including the database) to read them without your private key. When you set a Vault Password, your notes are encrypted locally with AES-256 before upload. QuickPad operates on an honor system for ephemeral data—everything is built to leave no trace.
